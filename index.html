Who is Srila Prabhupada?

His Divine Grace A.C. Bhaktivedanta Swami Prabhupada 1896 – 1977

His Divine Grace A.C. Bhaktivedanta Swami Prabhupada (1896–1977) is the Founder-Acharya of the International Society for Krishna Consciousness (ISKCON). He presented the timeless wisdom of India's Vedic literature to the modern world with unexcelled clarity, devotion, and source-grounded authenticity.

Historical Milestones

His Journey and Legacy

An extraordinary spiritual movement that transformed millions of lives around the globe in just eleven years.

1896 – 1965

Preparation & Vrindavan Years

After meeting his Guru Maharaj in Kolkata (1922), Srila Prabhupada published Back to Godhead magazine and translated the first cantos of Srimad-Bhagavatam at Sri Radha-Damodara temple in Vrindavan.

View Early Timeline → Source: Lilamrta ↗
1965

Jaladuta Voyage to the West

At age 69, aboard the steamship Jaladuta, Srila Prabhupada endured two heart attacks during a perilous 38-day sea journey to bring Chaitanya Mahaprabhu's message to America.

Read Jaladuta Story → Source: ISKCON History ↗
1966

Incorporation of ISKCON

On July 11, 1966, Srila Prabhupada legally incorporated the International Society for Krishna Consciousness at 26 Second Avenue, New York City, launching a global spiritual movement.

Explore Historic Centers → Source: GBC Record ↗
1966 – 1977

Literary Masterpieces & World Tours

Completed 14 world tours, authored over 80 volume translations of scriptures including Bhagavad-gita As It Is, and initiated thousands of disciples into pure Bhakti.

ABOUT THIS INITIATIVE

Preserving & Sharing a Transcendent Legacy

His Divine Grace A.C. Bhaktivedanta Swami Prabhupada carried the eternal wisdom of Vedic culture across the globe, translating over 80 volumes of sacred scriptures and founding a worldwide spiritual movement. Prabhupada Seva is a dedicated digital learning resource created to organize, preserve, and present his extraordinary life, profound books, lectures, conversations, and historical memoirs in an accessible, respectful, and structured format.

🌐

Why This Digital Resource Exists

In an era of fragmented online information, spiritual seekers require a reliable, unified space. This platform serves as a focused repository where Srila Prabhupada's authentic message can be studied with clarity, reverent presentation, and deep contextual understanding.

📜

Preserving Life & Teachings

We systematically present his monumental translation works—such as Bhagavad-gītā As It Is and Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam—alongside documented historical timelines, audio recordings, letters, direct conversations, and authentic memoirs of his global sankirtana mission.

📖

What Visitors Can Discover

Visitors can explore the profound philosophy of Gaudiya Vaishnavism, study authentic PDF literature, examine key historical milestones, listen to devotional sound vibrations, and receive practical spiritual guidance for daily life.

🌱

Serving Every Level of Inquiry

Designed thoughtfully for practicing devotees deepening their sadhana, academic researchers studying Vedic philosophy, students of world religions, and curious newcomers seeking genuine spiritual direction.

🛡️

Fidelity to Authentic Sources

Every record, timeline event, and reference is carefully grounded in primary source materials—Srila Prabhupada's original books, lectures, correspondence, and verified historical archives—ensuring maximum historical accuracy and spiritual integrity.
