    Content & Source Policy

    This project exists to help visitors learn about Srila Prabhupada, his life, teachings, and legacy with reverence, clarity, and care.

    Required record for every content item

    Each timeline item, story, quotation, article, image, video, and audio link must record: title and category; source title, author or speaker, and stable link or reference; source type (primary text, lecture, conversation, official institution, biography, or devotee account); exact quotation location when a quotation is used; and reviewer, review date, and status (Draft, Verified, or Published).

    How content is labelled

    Historical fact — supported by an identified reliable source. Devotee account / remembrance — attributed to the person or book, not presented as universally established history. Reflection / lesson — a clearly labelled explanation for readers, not a statement made by Srila Prabhupada unless directly cited. Draft — never public as verified material.

    Source preference

    Use Srila Prabhupada's original books, letters, lectures, and conversations first where relevant. For biography and institutional history, prefer official ISKCON, GBC, BBT, Vedabase, and reliable archival or biographical sources. Wikipedia may help locate references but is not a final authority.

    Conflicting information

    Do not hide disagreements. Add each source, describe the difference briefly, and keep the item in "Needs review" until Tanvi approves the final wording.

    Copyright and images

    Do not copy books, recordings, or photographs without permission. Prefer brief quotations with references and links to authorised reading, listening, or purchase pages. Record image creator, rights status, and credit before publishing.

    How each story will be labelled

    Historical fact, devotee account, and reflection will be visibly distinct. Readers will always be able to see the source.

    Lila research template

    For every lila, Tanvi please fill in as much of this as you can — even short notes help. Aayush will format it for the site.

    • Title — short name for the story
    • Summary — 1-2 sentence overview
    • Full story — the complete account, in your own words
    • Time / place — when and where it happened (approximate is fine)
    • Source — book, lecture, conversation, or devotee's name/account it came from
    • Exact reference — chapter, page, date, or timestamp if you have it
    • Lesson / reflection — optional, clearly marked as reflection, not fact
    • Status — Draft / Needs review / Verified / Published

    Raw data format (for reference — Aayush uses this on the technical side):

    {
  "title": "",

  "body": "",
  "period": { "startYear": null, "endYear": null, "display": "" },

  "sources": [{ "title": "", "authorOrSpeaker": "", "sourceType": "biography", "url": "", "reference": "", "quoteVerified": false }],
  "reflection": "",
  "researcher": "Tanvi",
  "reviewer": "",
  "status": "draft"
